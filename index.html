Welcome

Welcome to San Tan Hot Rods & Restorations!

Directly beside San Tan Auto Body, San Tan Hot Rods & Restorations is the specialty body shop owned and operated by the Smith family.

On the hot rod side, we’re all about classics and making your ride amazing! With over 50 years’ combined experience, the Smiths have restored, rebuilt, and repaired hundreds of classic cars and trucks for many happy clients.

Stop by to say hello and check out one of several projects we have on the go at any given time. We know you’ll be impressed!

About Us
What we do

Services

Body work, paint, mechanical, chassis, fabrication, and digital dashes, all under one roof.

01

Body Work

Here at San Tan Hot Rods we repair and/or replace aluminium, steel, and fiberglass. Rust is never a problem here! We can either repair a panel with rust or replace the panel if it is too bad to repair.

02

Paint

Our restoration shop uses PPG paint base coat/clear coat: custom single colors, custom multi colors, custom flames, and much more! Plus, PPG paint is warrantied for life by the manufacturer.

03

Mechanical & Electrical

If your ride is suffering from electrical or mechanical issues, we can troubleshoot it and repair it. Regardless of the cause, whether it’s from being flooded, from the incessant sun beating down on it, or you were in a collision, we can repair or replace the problem.

04

Suspension & Chassis

Suspension repairs or replacement, whether it’s for your hot rod or on your Cadillac, are something we do on a regular basis. From ground-up restorations to collision repairs, we haven’t met a project we couldn’t do.

05

Fabrication

Our restoration technicians have over 50 years’ experience in fabrication work. Anything from a custom cup holder to a fabricated custom dash, we strive to make sure you get exactly what you’re looking for on your vehicle.

06

Digital Dashes

Has your old dash stopped working and you don’t want to go too high-tech? We have options for keeping the retro look, with the quality of today’s technology.
