Our story

Welcome to San Tan Hot Rods & Restorations!

Directly beside San Tan Auto Body, San Tan Hot Rods & Restorations is the specialty body shop owned and operated by Corey Smith.

On the hot rod side, we’re all about classics and making your ride amazing! With over 50 years of combined family experience, the Smith family has restored, rebuilt, and repaired hundreds of classic cars and trucks for many happy clients. From simple collision repairs to full frame-off restorations, San Tan Hot Rods has the shop, staff, and ability to build your dream car.

Stop by to say hello and check out one of several projects we have on the go at any given time. We know you’ll be impressed!

Core values
“You Dream it, we’ll Build it”

Customer service excellence has always been and will always be one of the critical competitive advantages of our business. We earn a new customer, not a sale.

We will always go above and beyond to ensure your dream car or truck is even better than what you imagined it would be.
