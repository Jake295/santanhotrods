You’re at the right place

Collision Repair to Frame-Off Restorations

Were you involved in a collision and your car needs repairs? Do you have some minor things bugging you that you know you should get looked at? Or perhaps you’re finally ready to restore that classic you have sitting in the garage that’s been collecting dust for the past 30 years? You’re at the right place!

Schedule your vehicle maintenance or repairs right here. Our top-notch service staff can get your car or truck in and out quickly. We know there isn’t much more frustrating than being without your vehicle while it gets repaired. That’s why we have great staff that excel in providing top-notch service repairing your vehicle, be it collision repairs, suspension rebuilds, or a complete frame-off restoration. We’re confident one of the many services we offer will meet your needs, and we do it quickly and efficiently.

Our services

Built Right, Top to Bottom

01

Body Work

Here at San Tan Hot Rods we repair and/or replace aluminium, steel, and fiberglass. Rust is never a problem here! We can either repair a panel with rust or replace the panel if it is too bad to repair.

We always will do what it takes to ensure your vehicle is restored properly and just as you dreamed it to be!

02

Paint

Our restoration shop uses PPG paint base coat/clear coat: custom single colors, custom multi colors, custom flames, and much more! Plus, PPG paint is warrantied for life by the manufacturer.

We always ensure your vehicle is finished to the highest standards so there aren’t any imperfections once we’ve painted. This means a clean, smooth surface fully prepped prior to hitting the paint booth.

03

Mechanical & Electrical

If your ride is suffering from electrical or mechanical issues, we can troubleshoot it and repair it. Regardless of the cause, whether it’s from being flooded, from the incessant sun beating down on it, or you were in a collision, we can repair or replace the problem.

Our expert team of mechanics diagnoses problems quickly and accurately to ensure that your car is fixed properly; no guessing.

04

Suspension & Chassis

Suspension repairs or replacement, whether it’s for your hot rod or on your Cadillac, are something we do on a regular basis. From ground-up restorations to collision repairs, we haven’t met a project we couldn’t do.

Sub-frames and suspension components are provided by Full Tilt. We do all things chassis, from frame-off restoration to simple repairs.

05

Fabrication

Our restoration technicians have over 50 years’ experience in fabrication work. Anything from a custom cup holder to a fabricated custom dash, we strive to make sure you get exactly what you’re looking for on your vehicle.

We have completed many ground-up, frame-off restorations and have many happy customers.

06

Digital Dashes

Has your old dash stopped working and you don’t want to go too high-tech? We have options for keeping the retro look, with the quality of today’s technology.

Electronic instrument clusters, including speedometers: we handle all the work related to digital dashes, making sure yours works exactly the way it should. Come chat with us to check out your options.
